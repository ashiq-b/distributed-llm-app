## Android hosts, the Mac app is the worker

1. Mac app set to Worker.
2. On Android: Devices, switched "Ashiq's MacBook Air" on, then Chat, Load Qwen3.5 0.8B.

Results:
- llama.cpp on Android: `offloaded 21/26 layers to GPU` and `RPC0[192.168.1.7:50052] model buffer size = 657.51 MiB`. Android holds `CPU_REPACK 105.85 MiB` of layer weights, plus the token embeddings in `CPU_Mapped`.
- The app shows **This device 0–4, Ashiq's MacBook Air 5–25**, "On Ashiq's MacBook Air and this device" ([screenshot](5.2/android-hosts-split.png)).
- Chat on Android:
  - "What is the capital of France? Answer in one sentence." → "The capital of France is Paris." (7 tokens in 1.2 s, 5.7 tokens/s)
  - Follow-up "And of Italy?" → "The capital of Italy is Rome." (7 tokens in 0.9 s, 8.0 tokens/s)
  - [Screenshot](5.2/android-hosts-chat.png)
- Hosting alone, the app shows "On this device" and "This device 0–25" ([screenshot](5.2/android-hosts-alone.png)).

## Found and fixed during this run

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| An Android host gave **all 26 layers** to the Mac and ran none itself. llama.cpp gives every layer to "GPU" devices, and a phone without Metal has only the CPU. | `engine/cpp/dl_engine.cpp`: with no local GPU, the workers get layers in proportion to free memory (`<arch>.block_count` from the GGUF header), the first layers stay on this device's CPU, and the CPU is listed as this device's share. Mac and iPhone unchanged. |
| This device's share left out its CPU weights (`CPU_REPACK`) | `dl_stats.cpp` counts `_REPACK` buffers with their device |
| Tapping Send with the keyboard up did nothing: the field lost focus, the keyboard closed mid-tap, and the button moved under the finger | Send and Stop don't take focus. Covered by a test. |
| After an answer, the keyboard covered the composer | The keyboard's height (`--inset-keyboard`) is kept clear below the page ([screenshot](5.2/android-keyboard-composer.png)) |
| After the back gesture closed the keyboard, the tabs stayed hidden | On Android "typing" means the keyboard is actually up |
| Cancelling a download showed a red "download cancelled" error | Cancel is not an error. Covered by a test. |
| The top device label in Chat's mesh was cut off | Labels stay inside the view |

## Open

**Three-device split (the iPhone hosts with the Mac and Android as workers), and the iPhone seeing Android.** For the iPhone to reach the emulator's worker, the forwarded port has to be opened on the Mac's Wi‑Fi address (`adb -a` or a relay). That exposes the emulator to the network and was not done without the owner's go-ahead. It also needs hands on the iPhone. With a real Android phone none of this is needed, since it is directly on the Wi‑Fi.
