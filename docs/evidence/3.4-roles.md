## Seen on device, not a bug

After the Mac app was reopened, the Mac's list was empty while the iPhone listed the Mac. That matches the current rule: only workers announce themselves, and the iPhone was hosting, not a worker.

## Found on device, for a later task

With 512 tokens per answer, LFM3 and Qwen3.5 sometimes spend the whole budget inside their `<think>` reasoning. The answer then ends with `stopped: max_tokens`, and after the reasoning is removed it is empty. Running the same question through `llama-cli` on the Mac shows both models start with long reasoning. Handling it (thinking control, a longer limit, showing partial reasoning) is a separate task.
